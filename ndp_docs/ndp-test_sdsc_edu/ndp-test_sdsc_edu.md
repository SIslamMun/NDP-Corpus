## What is the National Data Platform?
The National Data Platform, or NDP, is a federated and extensible data ecosystem to promote collaboration, innovation, and use of data on top of existing cyberinfrastructure capabilities.
![ndp-infographic](https://wagtail-test.nationaldataplatform.org/media/original_images/ndp-infographic.png)

## Use and create data and AI services

## The National Data Platform: Increasing Access for Educational Innovation
The NSF-funded National Data Platform (NDP) is a cloud-compatible collaborative hub that removes barriers to robust and well-organized datasets and provides easy-to-use educational workflows to utilize high-performance computing resources from anywhere in the country.
